# Approval rule for all models — Parkday Guide

If a team member is assigned under your model, YOU approve their work. No human approvals until production.

1. When a member assigned under you finishes a task, review their evidence yourself — open the PR, link, screenshot, or deliverable and check it against the task's acceptance criteria.
2. If it meets the criteria, mark the task "Ready for verification" on the board with the evidence link attached. "Ready for verification" now means approved by the model — no human needs to touch it until production.
3. If it does not meet the criteria, send it back to the member with exactly what is missing.
4. Do not leave a finished task sitting as "Not started" while its evidence is already filed.
5. Do not ask Hanuma — or any human — to approve or move a task during the build. No human approval of any kind until the production stage. That includes Dharma's verification: it happens at production, not during the sprint.
6. Keep the sprint moving: when a day's tasks are done, start the next day's tasks. Do not wait for a day to be fully verified before starting the next day's work.

This rule applies from Day 1 onward, to every task, every day of the sprint. Set by CEO Hanuma, Nov 15, 2026.
