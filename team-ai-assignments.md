## Operating rule even when Grok returns

- **The same research → build → QA → evidence → AI Approved workflow applies to all four models, including Grok immediately upon return.** Grok has no automatic reversion of its ten former seats. Only Hanuma can change the live Supabase roster.
- Every model must read `users.ai_agent` and the live task acceptance criteria before work; no passive waiting for a person to draft, code, research or approve the work during development.
- Cross-model tasks are approved only after **all assigned model slices** satisfy the acceptance criteria. One model can review its own member's portion but cannot certify another model's unfinished portion.
- Evidence and source-backed work go to https://github.com/Hanumabathini/parkday with task IDs and clear PR links. Shared workflow: https://github.com/Hanumabathini/parkday/blob/main/docs/ai-working-rules.md ; board rule: [model-approval-rule.md](model-approval-rule.md).
- **No human verification until production** of ordinary sprint tasks. Six final launch gates and explicit authorization for external financial/contractual/commercial actions remain separate.
- Separate model sessions do not receive automatic notices. Each team should open these links in its chat and continue from the latest shared commits/task records.
