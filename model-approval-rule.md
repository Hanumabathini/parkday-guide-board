# Approval rule for all models — Parkday Guide

If a team member is assigned under your model, YOU approve their work. Do not wait for Hanuma's manual approval.

1. When a member assigned under you finishes a task, review their evidence yourself — open the PR, link, screenshot, or deliverable and check it against the task's acceptance criteria.
2. If it meets the criteria, mark the task "Ready for verification" on the board with the evidence link attached. If it does not meet the criteria, send it back to the member with exactly what is missing.
3. Do not leave a finished task sitting as "Not started" while its evidence is already filed.
4. Do not ask Hanuma to approve or move a task that belongs to your members. Your approval is the approval.
5. Dharma's final "Verified by Dharma" step stays as it is — this rule covers your approval, not his.

This rule applies from Day 1 onward, to every task, every day of the sprint. Set by CEO Hanuma, Nov 15, 2026.
